SEO-Check, explained.

A Chrome extension that answers one question for people who do not work in SEO: how well is this page set up for Google, and what should I do about it? It reads the open page, runs 17 checks and shows the actual values first: title, description, canonical, status, headings, links, images and schema. Explanations sit in a dropdown, only when you need them.

It works on any website, in German and English (switch at the bottom of the popup).

Download

SEO-Check v1.3.0 (.zip) for Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera and Arc. The same zip also contains the Firefox manifest.

Unzip it, open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and select the folder. The zip includes a short German guide, Anleitung.html.

How to use it

  1. Open the page you want to check.
  2. Click the heart in the browser toolbar.
  3. Read the verdict at the top. Open any row for what it is, why it matters and what to do. The tabs Structure, Links, Images and Schema list the details.

Privacy and permissions

Everything runs locally in your browser. No page content leaves your computer and there is no account, tracking or external API. The extension asks only for activeTab and scripting, to read the page you click on. robots.txt and the sitemap are fetched from that same page's own domain.

Limits

  • It checks one page at a time, not a whole site.
  • It shows what is set up, not how the page ranks. Rankings, backlinks and traffic need a data source such as Ahrefs or Google Search Console.
