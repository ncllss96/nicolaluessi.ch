About me

My work sits between product, growth and operations. I turn ambiguous audience and workflow problems into testable product ideas, then carry the useful ones into everyday use.

At Tamedia, that means leading a portfolio across Product, Data, Engineering and Editorial, shaping platform capabilities, CMS-native products, analytics systems and human-in-the-loop AI workflows. I work from discovery and requirements through launch, evaluation and operational handover.

My route into product was not conventional. I co-founded Hockey News Schweiz, built its social audience and sold advertising across its channels. I later co-founded and operated Ballers across e-commerce, paid acquisition, B2B agreements, marketing and customer operations. The product was never the starting point. The audience was.

I am based in Switzerland and work in German, English and French. I prefer clear thinking, direct feedback and work that remains useful after the project is over.

Beyond work

I’m the third of four kids and grew up in Habsburg, a village of fewer than 500 people that shares its name with the castle above it and the dynasty that ruled large parts of Europe for centuries. I loved living somewhere that small with a name that big. I’m an Aargauer through and through, white socks and all.

My love of travelling came from my family, who were always heading off somewhere. On frequent childhood trips to New England, I got hooked on American sports. As a sports-crazed boy, I saw Tom Brady’s face everywhere. That was enough to make me a Patriots fan, and a Red Sox fan by extension, for life.

At 17, I had the privilege of spending a year in Revelstoke, British Columbia, as an exchange student and fell for the place completely. Some of the people I met there are still among my closest friends. I return most years to see them and to ski.

One of my life goals is to visit all seven continents, Antarctica above all, and to ski on as many of them as I can. I prefer travelling with as much freedom as possible, often in an RV. That is how I ended up proposing to my wife on a hike in Kluane National Park, feeling about as far from anywhere as you can get.
